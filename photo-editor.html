About Photo Preview Overlay Editor

A lightweight browser-based image tool for creating a video-style photo preview. Upload an image, customize the preview overlay, export a PNG, and open Shortlink V2 to create or manage a short link.

  • Photo upload and instant browser preview
  • Play button, REC, HD, time and progress overlays
  • Adjustable opacity and play-button size
  • Show or hide individual overlay elements
  • PNG export
  • Quick access to Shortlink V2
